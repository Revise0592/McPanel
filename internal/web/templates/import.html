Import a server

Upload a zip of a server folder from another host, or of a single world. McPanel works out the type and version, then lets you check everything before importing.

  • Works with Paper, Spigot, Bukkit, Purpur, Fabric, Vanilla and Bedrock servers.
  • Worlds, settings, plugins, mods and player lists are kept.
  • A zip of just a world (Java world folder or Bedrock .mcworld contents) creates a new server around it.
