<p>McPanel is a self-hosted web panel for running Minecraft servers. This app connects to your own panel so you can keep an eye on your servers from your phone.</p>
<p>This is an early version. It can:</p>
<ul>
<li>Pair with your panel using the code from its settings</li>
<li>Show which servers are up, who's playing, and CPU, memory and TPS</li>
</ul>
<p>Coming next:</p>
<ul>
<li>Scanning the pairing QR code</li>
<li>Start, stop and restart servers</li>
<li>Live console, and sending commands</li>
<li>Notifications when a server crashes or something fails</li>
</ul>
<p>The app only talks to the panels you pair it with. It has no ads, no tracking and no Google services.</p>
