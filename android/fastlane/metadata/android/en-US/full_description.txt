<p>McPanel is a self-hosted web panel for running Minecraft servers. This app connects to your own panel so you can keep an eye on your servers from your phone.</p>
<p>It can:</p>
<ul>
<li>Pair with your panel using the code or QR code from its settings, at home or through an HTTPS reverse proxy</li>
<li>Show which servers are up, who's playing, and CPU, memory and TPS</li>
<li>Start, stop, restart and wake servers</li>
<li>Show the live console and send commands</li>
<li>Kick, op and ban players, and manage the whitelist</li>
<li>Show each server's activity</li>
<li>Use each panel's own accent colour, theme and logo</li>
</ul>
<p>Coming next: notifications when a server crashes or something fails.</p>
<p>The app only talks to the panels you pair it with. It has no ads, no tracking and no Google services.</p>
